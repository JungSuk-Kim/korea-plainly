EXPLORE KOREA

Regions

Discover Korea through regional travel, food, cafes, shopping, culture, leisure and stay recommendations.

Seoul

Korea's capital mixes royal palaces and hanok alleys with neon shopping streets, riverside parks and some of the best food in the country.

Busan

Korea's second city sits on the sea: beaches at Haeundae and Gwangalli, a huge fish market, hillside villages and temples on the cliffs.

Jeju

A volcanic island off the south coast with black-rock beaches, crater hikes, tangerine farms and a slower pace than the mainland.

Gyeongju

The capital of the ancient Silla kingdom, often called a museum without walls: royal tombs, Bulguksa Temple and a lakeside palace site that glows at night.

Jeonju

Home to Korea's largest hanok village and a city famous for its food, especially bibimbap.

Gangneung

A seaside city on the east coast known for its beaches, sunrise views, coffee street and historic houses.

Incheon

The gateway to Korea, home to the main international airport, a historic port with Korea's oldest Chinatown, and islands reachable by ferry.

Sokcho

A small east coast port town at the foot of Seoraksan National Park, with fresh seafood, a lively market and one of Korea's most loved mountain hikes.

Yeosu

A harbour city on the south coast with island views, a cable car over the sea and night views of the bay.

Daegu

A big inland city with traditional medicine markets, mountain temples nearby and a busy food scene.

Daejeon

A science and research city in the middle of the country, known for its bakeries, hot springs at Yuseong and easy rail links in every direction.

Gwangju

The cultural capital of the southwest, known for its role in Korea's democracy movement, its art biennale and the generous Jeolla-style food.
