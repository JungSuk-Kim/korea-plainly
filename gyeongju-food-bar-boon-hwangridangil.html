What Bar Boon is

Bar Boon is a cocktail bar on Poseok-ro, in Hwangnidan-gil, one of Gyeongju’s best-known streets for eating, browsing and going out. It offers a change of pace from the city’s daytime sights: settle in for a drink, enjoy the bar atmosphere and give your feet a rest before heading back out or calling it a night. The venue has a Google rating of 5 from 954 reviews.

Hwangnidan-gil brings together cafés, restaurants, shops and bars in an area where older-style streetscapes sit alongside newer businesses. It is a popular place to wander, especially when you want to experience Gyeongju beyond its archaeological sites and museums. Bar Boon suits an evening when you are looking for a cocktail stop rather than another full meal.

If you are new to drinking in Korea, a cocktail bar is a straightforward choice: you can order a drink for yourself rather than needing to share a bottle or follow the group-drinking customs common at some Korean restaurants. Go with a companion or take a quiet break on your own; either way, the idea is to enjoy the drink at an unhurried pace.

What to order and how to enjoy the visit

Choose a cocktail that suits your taste, or tell the staff what flavours and spirits you usually like and ask for a recommendation. If you have a preferred level of sweetness or strength, mention it when ordering. That simple bit of guidance can make it easier to choose when you are unfamiliar with the menu. If you would rather not drink alcohol, ask whether a non-alcoholic option is available.

There is no need to turn a bar visit into a complicated itinerary. Come in after dinner for a change of scene, or make it your main stop when you want a drink without planning a long night out. Take time to look around and enjoy the atmosphere instead of rushing through your order. If you are travelling with others, agree on how you will get back to your accommodation before the evening gets late.

As in any Korean bar, be considerate of the people around you and keep your voice at a comfortable level. If you are sharing a table with friends, order for yourself and check with the group before adding anything to a shared bill. A polite greeting and a simple “thank you” are always welcome; “gamsahamnida” means thank you in Korean.

What to see around Hwangnidan-gil

The street itself is part of the outing. Browse its cafés and small shops, pause for a meal, then settle in at Bar Boon when you are ready for an evening drink. Hwangnidan-gil is also well placed for a day focused on central Gyeongju’s historic landscape. Daereungwon, the royal tomb park, is a familiar landmark in this part of the city, while Cheomseongdae, the ancient astronomical observatory, is another popular stop to include in a walk around central Gyeongju.

Gyeongju’s character comes from the contrast between those open historic sites and the busier streets where people eat and meet. You can spend the day visiting monuments and still have a lively neighbourhood to explore afterward. Keep in mind that the historic attractions and the bar are different kinds of stops: plan sightseeing for the daytime, then leave Hwangnidan-gil for a meal or drink when you want a more social evening.

How to plan your visit

Bar Boon works best as part of an evening in Hwangnidan-gil. Allow enough time to enjoy a drink without squeezing it between tightly timed sightseeing stops; about an hour is a reasonable starting point, with more time if you are making an evening of the neighbourhood. The best time of day is after your daytime visits, when you are ready to eat, browse or relax. If you prefer a quieter outing, consider arriving earlier in your evening rather than treating the bar as a late-night destination.

For a simple plan, spend the day exploring Gyeongju’s historic centre, have dinner around Hwangnidan-gil and finish with a cocktail at Bar Boon. You can also reverse that order and stop in for a drink before dinner. The area is popular, so keep your plans flexible rather than relying on a strict schedule. Use the full address, 56-ho, 1058-7 Poseok-ro, Gyeongju, Gyeongsangbuk-do, when navigating; the name of the street is also commonly written as Hwangnidan-gil.

Good to know

Bar Boon is a drinks stop, not a sightseeing attraction or a place to plan a full meal around. If you are visiting with children or someone who does not drink, ask the staff what options are available before settling in. Carry a translation app if you want help discussing a particular flavour, ingredient or preference, and show the address on your phone if you need help finding the entrance. Check current hours and prices on Naver Map before you go.

FAQ

Where is Bar Boon?

It is at 56-ho, 1058-7 Poseok-ro in Gyeongju, in the Hwangnidan-gil neighbourhood.

Is Bar Boon a restaurant?

It is a cocktail bar, so plan it as a place for drinks rather than as your main meal stop.

What can I pair with a visit to Bar Boon?

Explore central Gyeongju’s historic sights during the day, then have dinner or browse around Hwangnidan-gil before or after your drink.
