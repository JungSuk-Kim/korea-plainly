Regional hub

Daejeon

A science and research city in the middle of the country, known for its bakeries, hot springs at Yuseong and easy rail links in every direction.

Planning a trip to Daejeon

Getting there

Daejeon is about an hour from Seoul by KTX, with two main stations, Daejeon and Seodaejeon.

How long to stay

A day trip or one night is enough for the science museums, Hanbat Arboretum and the city's famous bakery.

Getting around

A subway line and buses cover the city; taxis are easy to find.

When to go

Any season; spring and autumn are the best for the parks and arboretum.

Where to stay

Stay near Daejeon Station or in the Dunsan area for restaurants and transport.
