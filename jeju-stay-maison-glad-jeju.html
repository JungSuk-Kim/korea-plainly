What it is

Maison GLAD Jeju is a full-service hotel in Yeondong, a busy neighbourhood in Jeju City. Its central location makes it a practical base if you want restaurants, cafes and shops close by, or if you plan to explore the city as part of a wider island trip. It is a city hotel, rather than a beach resort: choose it for convenience and facilities, not for a seaside setting.

The hotel has restaurants and cafes, along with a spa and casino. Its rooms have a clean, minimalist style, while the range of on-site amenities gives you options for relaxing or eating without heading back out. Google gives the hotel a rating of 4.2 from 4,174 reviews.

What to see, do or order

Make time to enjoy the hotel itself. After a day of sightseeing, the spa offers a way to unwind, and the hotel’s restaurants and cafes make it easy to take a break without planning another outing. Dining choices can change, so check what is available during your stay before deciding where to eat.

If you want to try Jeju food, look for local specialties in Yeondong and elsewhere in the city. Black pork and seafood are popular choices on the island. You can also browse the neighbourhood for casual cafes and restaurants, which is a nice way to get a feel for everyday Jeju City beyond the hotel.

The casino is another on-site amenity, but it is not a must-do for every visitor. If you are curious, check its entry requirements before you go. Otherwise, treat the hotel as a comfortable base and spend your time exploring the city or taking day trips around Jeju.

How to plan your visit

Maison GLAD Jeju works well for an overnight stay or a longer city-based visit. Give yourself time to check in, settle into the room and use the facilities; if you plan to visit the spa or dine at the hotel, leave space in your schedule rather than treating it only as a place to sleep. Your overall stay can be as relaxed or as busy as your island itinerary.

Daytime is convenient for heading out to explore, while the evening is a good time to enjoy Yeondong’s restaurants and cafes or stay in for a meal. The area is also useful if you want to shop or have an easy meal close to your accommodation. For a city-focused day, combine a walk around Yeondong with a visit to Shilla Duty Free. Jeju’s traditional markets and coastal sights make worthwhile outings on other parts of your trip.

Jeju International Airport is in the same city, and the hotel’s location in central Jeju City can be handy at the beginning or end of an island itinerary. For trips beyond the city, consider how you want to get around: taxis and buses are options, while a rental car can make it easier to visit multiple sights across the island. Allow extra flexibility for travel between destinations, especially when your plans include places outside the urban area.

Good to know

The hotel is at 80 Noyeon-ro, Jeju-si, Jeju-do. Enter “Maison GLAD Jeju” or the Korean address into your preferred map app to guide a taxi or plan a route. In Korea, showing the destination on your phone can be useful when asking for directions.

Yeondong is a built-up city neighbourhood, so expect shops, dining and traffic rather than a quiet resort setting. If you are visiting Jeju mainly for beaches or rural scenery, plan your transport and day trips in advance, and use the hotel as a comfortable city base between outings. Check current facility details and dining availability before arranging your plans.

FAQ

Is Maison GLAD Jeju a good base for a first visit to Jeju?

It can be a convenient choice if you want to stay in Jeju City, with dining, cafes and hotel facilities nearby. Plan transport for island sights beyond the city.

Does the hotel have places to eat?

Yes. The hotel has restaurants and cafes. For other choices, Yeondong has restaurants and cafes within the surrounding neighbourhood.

Is Maison GLAD Jeju near the beach?

It is in central Jeju City, not a beachfront location. You will need to travel to reach Jeju’s coastal scenery and beaches.
