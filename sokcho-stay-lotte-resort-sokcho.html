What it is

Lotte Resort Sokcho is a polished hotel in Daepo-dong, on Sokcho’s coast. It suits travellers who want more than a place to sleep: you can settle in, enjoy the resort facilities and take in the sea without having to plan every part of your stay around outings. The hotel has dining, a gym and a rooftop bar, and its rooms are known for sea views.

The setting gives the stay a different feel from a hotel in the middle of town. You’re in the Daepo area, close to the harbour and the southern end of Sokcho, with the shoreline as a defining part of the experience. Google gives the resort a rating of 4.5 from 5,857 reviews.

For a first trip to Korea, a resort like this can be a useful balance: you have the comforts of a larger hotel, while Sokcho’s seafood, beaches and mountain scenery are all part of the wider destination. It works especially well for couples, families and anyone who wants downtime between sightseeing days.

What to see, do or enjoy

Start with the view. If your room looks out to sea, give yourself time to enjoy it rather than treating the room as somewhere to drop your bags. Sokcho faces east, so an ocean-facing room can be a lovely place to watch the morning light. Views vary by room, so check the room details when choosing what to book.

On site, the gym offers a way to keep up your routine, while the rooftop bar gives you a place to wind down with a drink and a view. The resort also has a water park, which can make staying in part of the holiday—particularly if you’re travelling with children. Check the resort’s current facility details before building your plans around any particular activity.

Outside the hotel, Daepo Port is a natural first stop. It’s a working harbour area where you can get a feel for Sokcho’s connection to the sea. For a beach walk, head towards Sokcho Beach; for a broader look at the town’s everyday life and local food, add Sokcho Tourist & Fishery Market. If you’re drawn to mountain landscapes, Seoraksan National Park makes a striking contrast with the coast. Abai Village is another worthwhile stop for a glimpse of Sokcho’s distinctive local history and food culture.

Rather than trying to fit every sight into one day, choose a mix: a harbour or market visit, time by the water, and either a mountain outing or a relaxed resort afternoon. That leaves room to enjoy the hotel as well as the city.

How to plan your visit

Plan on staying at least one night if you want time both to explore Sokcho and make use of the resort. Two nights give you more breathing room, especially if you’d like to include Seoraksan or a longer beach-and-town day. The best amount of time depends on whether you see the hotel mainly as a base or as part of the holiday itself.

Morning is a good time for a coastal walk or a mountain-focused outing, while later in the day suits a slower return to the resort and time to relax. If you’re hoping to enjoy a sea view, make that a reason to leave some unplanned time in your schedule. The rooftop bar is another option for ending the day on site.

The address is 186 Daepohang-gil, Daepo-dong, Sokcho-si, Gangwon-do. When arranging a taxi, show the driver the Korean address or the resort name on your phone. You can pair your stay with Daepo Port and Sokcho Beach for an easy coastal theme, or use Sokcho as a base for a mix of sea, market and mountain scenery. The official website is useful for checking current room and facility information before you book.

Good to know

This is a resort-style hotel, so it’s a good fit if you value on-site amenities and a comfortable place to return to after exploring. If you plan to spend most of your time in central Sokcho, factor in the hotel’s location in the Daepo area when deciding how to get around. Korea’s larger resorts can have several facilities with their own schedules, so check current hours or prices on Naver Map before you go.

For meals, you can use the hotel’s dining options or head out to explore Sokcho’s local food scene. Seafood is a natural part of the coastal setting, but the town also offers plenty of reasons to wander beyond the harbour. Keep your plans flexible if the weather affects your time outdoors, and enjoy the resort facilities as a comfortable alternative.

FAQ

Is Lotte Resort Sokcho a hotel or a resort?

It is a hotel with resort-style facilities, including dining, a gym, a rooftop bar and a water park.

Is it a good choice for a first visit to Sokcho?

Yes. It offers a comfortable base for exploring the coast, harbour, market and nearby mountain scenery, while giving you facilities to enjoy on site.

What can I visit near the resort?

Daepo Port is in the surrounding area, and Sokcho Beach, Sokcho Tourist & Fishery Market, Abai Village and Seoraksan National Park are popular places to add to a Sokcho itinerary.
