FOOD · FIRST TRIP

A Beginner’s Guide to Korean Street Food

What common Korean street foods are, what they taste like and how visitors can order them.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Tteokbokki

Chewy rice cakes in a sweet and spicy sauce. It is one of Korea's best-known street foods.

Eomuk

Fish cake served on skewers, often with warm broth.

Hotteok

A sweet filled pancake, especially popular during colder weather.

Gimbap

Rice and fillings rolled in seaweed. It is common as a quick meal or snack.
