Planning a trip to Incheon

Getting there

Incheon is next to Seoul and home to Korea's main international airport. Subway Line 1 and the Suin-Bundang line link central Incheon with Seoul, and the AREX train runs between the airport and Seoul.

How long to stay

Half a day or a day is enough for Chinatown, the Fairy Tale Village and Wolmido, or Songdo's Central Park. It also makes a good first or last night close to the airport.

Getting around

The subway covers the main areas and the same T-money card works as in Seoul. Taxis are useful for the airport island and the coast.

When to go

Any time of year. Songdo and the waterfront are pleasant on spring and autumn evenings.

Where to stay

Choose the airport area or Unseo for early flights, Songdo for modern hotels, or central Incheon for easy access to Seoul.

What to see first

Incheon Chinatown, Korea's oldest, is the home of jjajangmyeon (black bean noodles), and the Fairy Tale Village next door has painted alleys. Wolmido has a seaside promenade and small amusement rides. Songdo Central Park, with its canal and modern skyline, is pleasant in the evening. Ganghwa Island to the north has dolmen sites and temples.

What to eat

Try jjajangmyeon and jjamppong (spicy seafood noodle soup) in Chinatown, and the Jjajangmyeon Museum there explains how the dish became a Korean favourite. Sinpo International Market is known for dakgangjeong, crispy sweet and spicy fried chicken. Seafood restaurants line the waterfront at Wolmido and Yeonan Pier.

Good to know

Incheon Airport has a transit tour programme for long layovers and a free Korean culture area, so even a short stop can include a little sightseeing. If you have an early flight, staying near the airport saves a long trip from central Seoul.
