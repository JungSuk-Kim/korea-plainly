What it is

Ribs of Legend Haeundae is a Korean restaurant on Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, the road running alongside one of Busan’s best-known beaches. Its name points to the main draw: Korean-style beef ribs. The restaurant is on the third floor at 277 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, so look for the building entrance rather than expecting a beach-level dining room.

Beef ribs make for a hearty Korean meal, often enjoyed as a shared dish. That makes this a good choice if you want to sit down with friends or family after sightseeing, rather than grab a quick snack between stops. The restaurant has a 4.8 Google rating from 2,412 reviews.

Haeundae has plenty of places to eat, from casual seafood spots to cafés and Korean barbecue restaurants. Ribs of Legend is a useful pick when you’re in the mood for a meat-centered meal in the beach district. It can also introduce first-time visitors to the Korean custom of gathering around a meal and sharing dishes at the table.

What to order and what to expect

Start with the beef ribs, the natural focus of a restaurant called Ribs of Legend. If you’re not sure how much to order, ask the staff for a recommendation based on the number of people in your group. This is especially helpful if you’re new to Korean barbecue or want to leave room for other dishes.

Korean meals commonly arrive with small side dishes, known as banchan, to share. You can eat these alongside the main dish, and it’s fine to ask what something is if you don’t recognize it. If you have dietary restrictions or avoid particular ingredients, explain that clearly when ordering; a translation app can make the conversation easier.

At Korean barbecue restaurants, diners may cook meat at the table or have it prepared for them, depending on the restaurant and the dish. Follow the staff’s lead, and avoid reaching across a hot grill. If you’re dining with a group, let everyone know before ordering whether you’re sharing. Rice and other additions can turn a meat-focused meal into a more filling dinner, but check the menu for what is available.

How to plan your visit

Build your meal around a Haeundae outing. The beach is the obvious companion: take a stroll along the shoreline, then head inland to eat. You can also pair lunch or dinner with a visit to Haeundae Traditional Market, where you’ll find a different, more casual side of the neighborhood’s food scene. Dongbaekseom, a coastal walking area at the western end of the beach, is another good stop if you want a walk before sitting down to eat.

For timing, choose a meal break when you can linger instead of squeezing dinner into a tight sightseeing schedule. A relaxed meal works particularly well after a beach walk. If your group is large or you’re visiting at a busy mealtime, plan a little flexibility into the evening. Check the current hours and prices on Naver Map before setting out.

Allow enough time to order, enjoy the meal, and share dishes at an easy pace. If you’re continuing to another attraction, remember that the restaurant is on the third floor and factor in time to find the entrance. The address is Busan, Haeundae, Haeundaehaebyeon-ro 277, third floor.

Good to know

Haeundae is a popular visitor district, so the streets around the beach can feel busy, especially during holidays and peak travel periods. A restaurant meal is a welcome chance to take a break from the crowds, but it’s wise to keep your plans flexible. If you have a particular dish or dietary need in mind, ask before ordering rather than assuming a side dish or sauce is suitable.

When dining in Korea, it’s normal to share food with your table, but you can ask for an individual serving if that suits you better. A simple “Is this for sharing?” can help clarify how a dish is intended to be eaten. If you’re unsure how to pronounce the restaurant’s name, showing its Korean name, 전설의우대갈비, on your phone can help you identify the destination.

FAQ

Where is Ribs of Legend Haeundae?

It is at 277 Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, on the third floor, in Haeundae, Busan.

What should I order?

Beef ribs are the restaurant’s natural specialty. Ask the staff how much to order for your group.

What can I visit nearby?

Haeundae Beach, Haeundae Traditional Market, and the coastal paths around Dongbaekseom are good options to pair with a meal.
