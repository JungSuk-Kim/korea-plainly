What it is

Four Seasons Hotel Seoul is a high-end city hotel at 97 Saemunan-ro in Jongno District. It brings together rooms and suites, restaurants and bars, a spa, a gym and three pools, making it more than a place to sleep between sightseeing stops. You can settle in for a full stay or make time to enjoy the hotel itself during a visit to the capital.

The setting is a useful one for first-time visitors. Jongno combines historic landmarks with busy office streets, and the hotel sits close to the Gwanghwamun area. That means palace visits and walks through central Seoul can fit naturally around time indoors. The hotel has a Google rating of 4.6 from 7,416 reviews.

Expect an upscale atmosphere rather than a small, character-filled guesthouse. If you want a broad range of facilities in one place, or a quiet retreat after a day navigating the city, the hotel’s mix of accommodation, dining and wellness amenities is a practical draw.

What to see, do or order

Start with the area beyond the hotel. Gwanghwamun Square is a natural stop for a first walk, with the grand avenue and historic setting giving you a strong sense of central Seoul. Gyeongbokgung Palace is nearby, so you can pair the hotel with a visit to one of the city’s best-known royal sites. Check the palace’s own schedule before you go, as access and activities can vary.

Back at the hotel, choose what suits the pace of your day. The pools, gym and spa give you options for exercise or relaxation without having to plan another outing. The hotel’s restaurants and bars can also make an easy evening plan, particularly after a full day outdoors. If you have a particular meal or dining style in mind, check the current restaurant options before settling on a plan.

For a first visit to Korea, the hotel can also serve as a comfortable reset point. Seoul days often involve plenty of walking, stairs and crowded public spaces. Taking a break before heading out again can make an ambitious itinerary feel more manageable. You do not need to fill every hour with sightseeing: a slower morning or a relaxed evening at the hotel can be part of the trip.

How to plan your visit

If you are staying overnight, plan around the experiences you care about most. Guests who want to use the spa, pool facilities or gym should leave room in the schedule instead of treating the hotel as somewhere to sleep only. For dining, consider whether you want to eat in after a long day or explore Seoul’s many neighbourhood restaurants; both approaches work well from this central location.

For sightseeing, a half-day around Gwanghwamun and Gyeongbokgung makes a straightforward pairing. You can spend more time in the palace district if museums, traditional architecture or a slower walk appeal to you. Another option is to explore the central streets and return to the hotel for a break before dinner. The best time of day depends on your plans: an earlier start suits palace sightseeing, while the hotel’s indoor facilities are useful when you want downtime later.

Build in flexibility for Seoul’s changing weather and busy streets. In warm or rainy conditions, indoor facilities and hotel dining become especially convenient. On a clear day, make the most of the surrounding landmarks, then return to the hotel when you are ready to rest. If you are arriving in Seoul for the first time, having a central base can also make it easier to organize your first few days before branching out to other districts.

For transport, use the full hotel name and address when checking directions or arranging a taxi: Four Seasons Hotel Seoul, 97 Saemunan-ro, Jongno District. Keeping the address saved in a map app is helpful, especially if you are moving between different parts of the city.

Good to know

This is a luxury hotel, so it is best suited to travellers who value a full-service stay and want facilities on site. It can also work well for a special-occasion visit or for travellers who prefer to return to a calm, comfortable base after busy days. If you are choosing between accommodation areas, Jongno is a strong fit for historic central Seoul; it may be less appealing if your main plans are concentrated in a faraway district.
