Joyang Textile: A Vast Old Factory Reimagined as a Café in Ganghwa

Published 13 October 2026

Photo: Yongseok Choi via Google Maps

At a glance: Joyang Textile (조양방직) is a café and exhibition-style space in a former textile factory in Ganghwa-eup, Incheon. Its industrial setting and vintage character make it a memorable stop for visitors exploring Ganghwa. Google lists it at 4.2 stars from 8,911 reviews; ratings and review counts can change.

A café with a factory past

Joyang Textile is not a typical compact coffee shop. It occupies a former textile factory, established in 1933, that has been adapted into a café and exhibition space. The old industrial setting is the main attraction: rather than hiding the building’s past, the venue makes its factory character part of the visit.

Inside, visitors can expect a setting shaped by vintage objects and the architecture of the former works. The combination gives the space a different feel from a purpose-built modern café and makes it appealing even to people who are more interested in historic buildings and photography than in café culture. Because the site is extensive, allow time to look around instead of planning only a quick drink stop.

The factory’s history is also a reminder that Ganghwa has a story beyond its coastal scenery and traditional heritage sites. Joyang Textile offers a contemporary way to encounter one piece of the area’s industrial past, though it is a repurposed venue rather than a formal museum visit.

What to expect during a visit

Think of Joyang Textile as a destination café: the setting is a major part of the experience, and visitors often come to explore the space as well as to sit down. The scale and photogenic details may suit travelers who enjoy taking their time, looking for interesting backdrops, or adding an atmospheric indoor stop to a day on Ganghwa.

It is worth keeping expectations flexible. This is a working café, and available food, drinks, seating, and visitor arrangements may change.
