Joyang Textile: A Factory Turned Café

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 13 October 2026

At a glance: Joyang Textile is a former factory turned café and exhibition space, worth visiting for its industrial character and eclectic interiors.

What Joyang Textile is

Joyang Textile, also known by its Korean name, Joyang Bangjik, is not a typical modern coffee shop. It occupies a former textile factory in Ganghwa-eup, on Ganghwa Island in Incheon. The factory dates to 1933 and has been adapted into a café and a place to explore displays and curiosities.

The setting is the main draw. Rather than smoothing away the building’s working past, the space makes a feature of its old-factory atmosphere. Weathered surfaces, industrial details and vintage objects give the rooms a lived-in, collected feel. It is an easy stop to combine a café break with a look around, especially if you enjoy photographing distinctive interiors.

Joyang Textile has a 4.2-star Google rating from 8,911 reviews. It is a popular stop on Ganghwa, so expect other visitors, particularly at busy times.

What to see, do or order

Take time to look beyond the café counter. The former factory’s architecture and the objects arranged throughout the space are part of the experience; there is no need to treat your visit as simply a quick coffee stop. Move through the different areas at a relaxed pace and notice how the old industrial setting contrasts with its present use.

Bring a camera or phone if you like taking pictures. The mix of aged building details and vintage décor gives you plenty to notice, though the atmosphere can be lively when the café is crowded. Be considerate of people sitting nearby, and avoid blocking walkways or other visitors’ photos.

Order from the café when you arrive and enjoy a pause between exploring. The specific menu can change, so choose from what is available on the day. If you are visiting with someone who is more interested in old buildings than cafés, the unusual setting gives you a reason to stop even if coffee is not the main event.

How to plan your visit

Allow roughly one to two hours if you want time to look around and sit down; a shorter stop works if you only want a drink and a quick look at the interiors. This is a flexible estimate rather than a required visit length. Going earlier in the day or on a weekday may make it easier to explore at your own pace, while weekends and holiday periods can bring more visitors.

Joyang Textile is at 12 Hyangnamu-gil 5beon-gil, Ganghwa-eup, Ganghwa, Incheon. Ganghwa Island is connected to the mainland by road, but plan your route for the whole island rather than assuming every attraction is an easy walk from the next. Check transport and directions for your starting point before setting out.

Make it part of a Ganghwa-eup day. Nearby, Ganghwa Anglican Church offers a very different look at the town’s history, while Ganghwa Pungmul Market is a good place to experience a local market atmosphere. If you are driving around the island, you can also build a wider outing around its historic sites and countryside. Give yourself room between stops rather than trying to rush through them all.

Good to know

Joyang Textile is both a café and a space to explore, so allow for a little wandering rather than expecting a conventional café layout. The old-building setting includes uneven visual textures and a variety of objects; take care as you move around and follow any signs or staff guidance. If you are visiting in a group, agree on a meeting point in case everyone explores at a different pace.
