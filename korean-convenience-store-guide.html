What to Expect at a Korean Convenience Store

Korean convenience stores are more than places to buy snacks. Here is what visitors should know about food, payment, microwaves, seating and everyday convenience-store culture.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

More than a place to buy snacks

Korean convenience stores are part of everyday life. You can buy drinks, snacks, ready-to-eat meals and basic daily necessities, often late into the night.

What can you buy?

  • Drinks, coffee and bottled water
  • Triangle kimbap and lunch boxes
  • Instant noodles and simple hot food
  • Snacks, desserts and ice cream

Can you eat there?

Many stores have a small seating area. Some also provide microwaves or hot-water stations for ready-to-eat food. Follow the store's signs and keep shared areas tidy.

Tip: Convenience stores are an easy backup when you need a quick meal, drink or small travel item.
