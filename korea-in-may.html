Korea in May: Weather, Holidays and What to Do

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 17 October 2026

Visiting Korea in May: warm sunny weather, the Lotus Lantern Festival for Buddha's Birthday, Children's Day crowds, roses and what to pack.

Photo: Ken Eckert, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

May is often the most comfortable month in Korea: warm and sunny, green everywhere, and not yet humid. It is also a month of holidays, including Buddha's Birthday with its beautiful lantern displays. Here is how to make the most of it.

On this page

  1. What the weather is like
  2. What to pack
  3. Things to do in May
  4. Holidays and busy dates
  5. FAQ

What the weather is like

Days in Seoul are warm and bright, and evenings are mild enough to sit outside. Late May can already feel like early summer. It is usually dry before the rainy season arrives in late June. Fine dust can still appear on some days, especially early in the month. See our seasons and weather guide.

What to pack

  • Light clothes plus a thin jacket or cardigan for evenings and air-conditioned places.
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat: the sun is strong.
  • Walking shoes for parks, palaces and hikes.

Things to do in May

Buddha's Birthday and the Lotus Lantern Festival

Buddha's Birthday falls on the eighth day of the fourth lunar month; in 2027 it is on 13 May. In the weeks before, temples hang thousands of colourful lanterns. In Seoul, the Lotus Lantern Festival (Yeondeunghoe) around Jogyesa temple and Insadong usually includes a huge lantern parade on a weekend before the day. On the day itself, many temples welcome visitors and some serve a free bowl of bibimbap. Check our festival calendar for this year's dates.

Roses and green hills

Roses bloom in late May, with rose festivals in several Seoul parks. It is also a lovely month for hiking, cycling along the Han River and day trips to the countryside.

Jeju and the coast

Jeju is green and pleasant in May, before the summer crowds; see our Jeju itinerary. Busan's beaches are good for walking, though the sea is still cool for swimming. See our Busan guide.

Holidays and busy dates

  • 1 May, Labour Day, and 5 May, Children's Day: theme parks, zoos and family attractions are very busy around these days.
  • Buddha's Birthday: temples are crowded but festive.
  • Long weekends: when holidays fall next to a weekend, trains and hotels fill up. Book KTX seats early; see our KTX guide.

Compare with Korea in April and Korea in June, or see every month in our best time to visit Korea guide.

FAQ

Is May a good time to visit Korea?

Yes. It is warm, sunny and green, before the summer heat and rain. Book ahead around the early-May holidays.

When is Buddha's Birthday in Korea in 2027?

On 13 May 2027. The Lotus Lantern Festival in Seoul usually takes place on a weekend shortly before it.

Is it hot in Korea in May?

Mostly warm and comfortable. Late May can feel like early summer, but it is not yet humid.
