Korea Packing List: What to Bring in Every Season (Interactive Checklist)

By Korea Plainly · Published 15 October 2026

What you pack for Korea depends mostly on the season: summers are hot and rainy, winters properly cold, and spring and autumn change a lot from morning to evening. A few things matter whatever the time of year, from the right plug adapter to socks you are happy to show off when you take your shoes off. Pick your season below and tick things off as you pack. Your checklist

Things people forget

  • A plug adapter. Korea uses the two round pin plugs (types C and F) at 220 volts. Most phone and laptop chargers work on any voltage; check the label on hair dryers and straighteners.
  • Mobile data. Sort out a SIM, eSIM or pocket Wi-Fi before you fly. See our SIM and eSIM guide.
  • The right map app. Google Maps directions are limited in Korea. See Naver Map vs Google Maps.
  • Good socks. You take your shoes off in many traditional restaurants, guesthouses and temples.
  • Room in your suitcase. Korean skincare, snacks and clothes are hard to resist. Claim your tax refund: see our tax refund guide.

What you can buy in Korea

Do not worry about forgetting small things. Convenience stores are open all night and sell toiletries, chargers, umbrellas, socks, face masks and heat packs, and pharmacies are easy to find in cities. See our convenience store guide.

For the weather in each season, see our seasons guide, and for the order to do everything else in, our first-trip planner.

FAQ

What plug do I need for Korea?

Korea uses types C and F, the two round pin plugs used in much of Europe, at 220 volts.

What should I wear in Korea?

Casual, comfortable clothes are fine almost everywhere. Dress in layers in spring and autumn, light clothes and an umbrella in summer, and a warm padded coat in winter.

Can I buy things I forgot in Korea?

Yes. Convenience stores, pharmacies and big supermarkets sell almost everything a traveller needs.
