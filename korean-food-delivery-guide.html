What to Know About Korean Delivery Food

By Korea Plainly · Published 9 October 2026

A practical guide to ordering Korean delivery food, from finding an app and entering your address to meeting the courier and sorting the packaging.

Photo: Julie Facine from Istanbul, Turkey, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Food delivery is a convenient way to try Korean meals without finding a restaurant or making a reservation. You can order everything from fried chicken and noodles to soups, rice dishes, and snacks. The process may feel unfamiliar, though: ordering apps often assume you have a Korean phone number, a Korean address, and a way to pay online. A little preparation can make the experience much easier.

Choose how to order

Delivery apps are the most common way to browse menus and place an order. Baemin (Baedal Minjok), Coupang Eats, and Yogiyo are well-known Korean services, but app access, language options, and payment requirements can vary. Some services may be difficult to use without a Korean phone number or locally accepted payment method. Check what works for you before relying on an app, and do not assume that an international card will be accepted.

If you are staying in a hotel or guesthouse, ask the front desk or host whether they can recommend a suitable ordering option or help with the address. Some places may not accept deliveries for guests, or may ask you to meet the courier outside. A local friend or host can also help you understand a Korean-language menu. Ordering directly from a restaurant by phone is possible, but it can be challenging if you do not speak Korean.

Get the address and delivery notes right

Enter the exact Korean address for your accommodation whenever possible. Copy it from your booking details or ask the host for the address written in Korean. A building name, entrance number, or room or unit detail may help the courier find you. A map pin is useful, but it may not show which entrance to use, especially in a large apartment complex or busy commercial building.

Use the delivery-instructions field to explain where to meet. Keep the note short and specific. If you can write Korean, include clear directions such as the building entrance or lobby. Otherwise, ask your host to help prepare a note. Do not share an entry code unless you are comfortable doing so and your accommodation allows it. Some buildings require visitors to call a resident or use an intercom.

Decide in advance whether you want to meet the courier at the entrance, lobby, or another permitted location. Deliveries to hotel rooms may not be allowed, and access to apartment buildings can be restricted. If you are unsure, ask the accommodation before placing your order.

Pick food that suits the moment

Menus often include photos, but the images may not show portion size or every ingredient. Look for dish names, options, and notes about spice or add-ons. Korean fried chicken, gimbap, dumplings, noodles, and rice bowls are common delivery choices. Soups and stews are popular too, though they may arrive in separate containers or need careful handling. If you have a food allergy or dietary restriction, do not rely on a photo alone. Ask someone who reads Korean to check the menu or contact the restaurant.

Some dishes travel better than others. Fried foods can soften in a closed container, while broth-based dishes may spill if handled roughly. If you are ordering for later, choose food that can be reheated safely and check whether the accommodation has a microwave. Read the order carefully before paying: options such as portion size, spice level, side dishes, and drinks can be easy to overlook.

Check payment and delivery details

Review the total, including any delivery fee shown at checkout, before confirming. Payment choices depend on the app and restaurant. Some orders require payment in the app; other payment options may be offered, but availability varies. If the app asks for a local phone number or rejects your card, try another ordering route rather than assuming the order went through.

After ordering, keep your phone nearby. A courier or restaurant may call or send a message to ask for directions or confirm the handoff. If you cannot understand the message, use a translation app or ask your host for help. Check the app for delivery updates, and make sure you can reach the meeting point you selected.

Receive the order and handle the packaging

When the courier arrives, be ready to collect the food promptly. You may need to meet them at the entrance instead of having the order brought to your room. Confirm that you have the right order before leaving the handoff point. If something is missing or damaged, use the app or restaurant contact details to report it as soon as you can; keep the packaging and order information until the issue is resolved.

Delivery meals often come with several containers, wrappers, and small side dishes. Sort the waste according to the rules at your accommodation or in your neighborhood. Instructions can differ by building and location, so ask your host where to put food waste, recyclables, and general rubbish. Do not assume all containers go in the same bin, and empty or rinse packaging if your accommodation requests it.

A few useful courtesies

  • Order to a place where you can receive the food. Avoid choosing an unfamiliar street corner unless you can identify it clearly.
  • Be ready to answer a call. Couriers may need help finding the entrance or confirming the drop-off point.
  • Follow the accommodation’s rules. Some hotels and buildings have specific instructions for delivery visitors.
  • Do not assume a tip is expected. Tipping is not generally expected for an ordinary delivery, but follow any clear instructions shown by the service.

Once you have confirmed the address, payment, and handoff plan, ordering delivery can be a simple way to enjoy local food at your accommodation. When in doubt, ask your host for help with the address or app before placing the order.

Bottom line: A little preparation can make your time in Korea much easier. Keep this guide handy and enjoy exploring at your own pace.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I don’t know how to enter my Korean address in a delivery app?

Use the full address provided by your accommodation, and check that the map pin points to the correct entrance. If the app is difficult to use, ask your hotel or host whether they can help place the order. Avoid entering a room number or other private details unless they are specifically needed for delivery.

Do I need a Korean phone number to order delivery?

It depends on the app or restaurant. Some services may ask for a local number or make it easier to communicate with the driver by phone. If you cannot complete an order, ask your accommodation for help or check whether the restaurant accepts orders directly. Keep your accommodation’s address handy.

What if I can’t use a delivery app in Korea?

Try asking your hotel or host for help, since they may know how to contact nearby restaurants or place an order. You can also look for a restaurant that accepts orders directly or choose takeaway if that is convenient. Options vary by location, so it is worth checking what is available nearby.
