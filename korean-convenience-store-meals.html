What Is a Korean Convenience Store Meal Like?

From triangle kimbap to instant noodles, here is how convenience-store meals work in Korea.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Triangle kimbap

Samgak kimbap is a convenient rice-and-filling snack wrapped in seaweed. It is one of the easiest Korean convenience-store foods for visitors to try.

Instant noodles

Many stores provide hot water and sometimes a microwave. Follow the package and store instructions.

Lunch boxes

Ready-made lunch boxes can include rice, meat, vegetables and side dishes. Selection changes throughout the day.

Tip: Check the preparation instructions before heating packaged food.
