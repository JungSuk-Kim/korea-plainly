Jeju Leports Land: Outdoor Kart Racing in Jeju

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 9 October 2026 · Updated 15 October 2026

At a glance: Jeju Leports Land is an outdoor go-kart venue where you can race a long track and add active attractions to a Jeju day.

What it is

Jeju Leports Land is a leisure and activity park in Jocheon-eup, on the northeastern side of Jeju Island. Its main attraction is go-karting: visitors drive around a dedicated outdoor track, making it a lively change of pace from Jeju’s beaches, museums and scenic walks. The setting is open-air, so the experience feels more like an active outing than an indoor amusement ride.

Karting is the central draw, but the park also offers other activities, including ziplining, a seasonal sled ride, shooting and off-road buggy experiences. The mix can make a visit appealing to families and groups with different ideas of fun. Google visitors give the venue a rating of 4.6 out of 5 from 4,621 reviews.

You’ll find it at 47 Waheulsangseo 2-gil, Jocheon-eup, Jeju-si. The address is useful to enter into a map app, especially if you’re driving around the island and want to navigate directly to the entrance.

What to do

Start with the kart track if racing is your priority. It gives you a chance to get used to the steering and pace before you settle into the ride. If you’re travelling with someone who would rather share the experience, ask about the available kart types and conditions when you arrive; solo and two-person options are offered. Staff can explain which choices suit your group.

For many visitors, the appeal is the chance to try more than one activity in the same outing. You might race first, then add another attraction if you still have energy. The park’s other activities bring a different kind of thrill: a zipline ride offers a view from above, while buggy experiences take you off the smooth track. The available options can vary, so check what is running on the day.

Bring comfortable clothes and shoes that stay secure while you move around. Follow the staff’s safety instructions and any posted rules before getting into a kart or joining another activity. If you’re visiting with children, check the requirements for each ride with staff rather than assuming that one attraction’s rules apply to all of them.

There are places to pause between activities, and the park has parking and restrooms. Build in a little breathing room if you’re visiting as a group: choosing activities, waiting your turn and taking photos can all add time to the outing.

How to plan your visit

Set aside a few hours if you want to try karting and explore more than one attraction. A shorter stop can work if you’re focused on the track, but allow extra time during busy periods or when travelling with children. The best time of day depends on your itinerary and the weather. Earlier in the day can leave you more flexibility for other stops, while a later visit may fit naturally after sightseeing. As the venue is outdoors, rain or strong wind may affect some activities.

A rental car is a convenient way to reach this part of Jeju and combine it with other stops. Jocheon is a good area to include in a day exploring the island’s northeast and interior. You could pair the activity park with Jeju Stone Park, which introduces visitors to Jeju’s volcanic landscape and stone culture, or with Eco Land, known for its forest train experience. Plan your route in a map app, since these are separate attractions rather than one connected site.

If you’re relying on public transport or taxis, plan the return trip as well as the journey there. Jeju is a large island, and activity stops outside the main urban areas can take more coordination than a walk between downtown attractions. Keep your next reservation or scheduled stop flexible enough to accommodate the time you spend at the park.

Good to know

Jeju Leports Land is best for visitors who want a hands-on activity, not a quiet sightseeing stop. It works well for families, friends and groups looking for something energetic between more relaxed island outings. You don’t need to be an experienced driver to enjoy a go-kart session, but you should be comfortable following instructions and sharing the track with other riders.

Wear clothes you can move in, and consider bringing a light layer if the weather is breezy. Keep valuables secure while riding, and use designated areas for photos rather than stopping on the track. If you’re visiting with a group, agree beforehand on which activities matter most so you can make the most of your time.

Check current opening hours and prices on Naver Map before setting out, as schedules and activity availability can change.

FAQ

Is Jeju Leports Land mainly a go-kart venue?

Yes. Go-karting is its main attraction, with additional activities such as ziplining, sledding, shooting and off-road buggy experiences.

Can I visit with children?

Families visit the park, but age, height or other participation requirements can differ by activity. Ask staff which options are suitable for your child before taking part.

What can I combine with a visit?

Jeju Stone Park or Eco Land can fit into a day exploring Jocheon and the island’s northeast. Check the route between stops and leave enough time for each attraction.
