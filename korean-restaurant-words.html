10 Korean Restaurant Words Worth Knowing

Published 7 October 2026

A short list of Korean food words that can make ordering and understanding a Korean restaurant menu much easier.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Start with a few words

You do not need to speak Korean fluently. Recognizing a handful of common words can make menus much easier to understand.

10 useful words

  • 밥 — bap — rice / meal
  • 면 — myeon — noodles
  • 국 — guk — soup
  • 찌개 — jjigae — stew
  • 고기 — gogi — meat
  • 돼지고기 — dwaejigogi — pork
  • 소고기 — sogogi — beef
  • 닭 — dak — chicken
  • 매운 — maeun — spicy
  • 물 — mul — water
Tip: If a menu has photos, combine the picture with one or two Korean words to confirm what you are ordering.
