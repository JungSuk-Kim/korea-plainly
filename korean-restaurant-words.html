10 Korean Restaurant Words Worth Knowing

Published 7 October 2026

Ten Korean words that make eating out easy, with pronunciation: calling the server, ordering, not spicy, refills, takeaway and paying the bill.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

You can eat very well in Korea without speaking Korean, but a handful of words makes every meal easier and earns a smile from the staff. These ten cover almost everything that happens in a restaurant, from getting attention to paying the bill. Pronunciation is given in simple English letters.

1. 저기요 (jeogiyo): excuse me

The word for getting a server's attention. Raise your hand and say it clearly. It is polite and completely normal; staff don't usually come to the table unless called. Some tables have a call button instead.

2. 이거 주세요 (igeo juseyo): this one, please

Point at the menu, a picture or another table's dish and say this. Juseyo means "please give me", and it works with almost any noun: mul juseyo is "water, please".

3. 메뉴판 (menyupan): menu

Ask "menyupan juseyo" if you haven't been given one, or "yeongeo menyu isseoyo?" (영어 메뉴 있어요?) to ask whether there is an English menu.

4. 물 (mul): water

Water is free, though often self-service. If you can't see it, ask "mul juseyo".

5. 1인분 (il-inbun): one portion

Inbun means a serving, and you will see it on menus next to prices. "I-inbun juseyo" (2인분 주세요) means "two portions, please", which is the usual minimum at barbecue restaurants.

6. 안 맵게 (an maepge): not spicy

Say "an maepge juseyo" to ask for a dish without spice, or "deol maepge" (덜 맵게) for less spicy. Not every dish can be changed, but it is always worth asking. "Maewoyo?" (매워요?) means "is it spicy?"

7. 더 주세요 (deo juseyo): more, please

For refills of side dishes, rice or drinks. Point at the empty plate and say it. Side dish refills are usually free.

8. 포장 (pojang): takeaway

"Pojang-iyo" (포장이요) means "to take away". "Pojang dwaeyo?" asks whether takeaway is possible, and "yeogiseo meogeulgeyo" means you will eat here. You will hear the same question in cafes.

9. 계산할게요 (gyesan halgeyo): I'd like to pay

Say this at the counter by the door, where most restaurants take payment. "Kadeu dwaeyo?" (카드 돼요?) asks if you can pay by card, which is almost always yes.

10. 잘 먹었습니다 (jal meogeosseumnida): thank you for the meal

Literally "I ate well". Say it as you leave or pay, and the staff will be delighted. A simple "gamsahamnida" (thank you) works too.

Bonus: three more useful words

  • 맛있어요 (masisseoyo): it's delicious.
  • 화장실 (hwajangsil): toilet. "Hwajangsil eodiyeyo?" asks where it is.
  • 알레르기 (allereugi): allergy. For a serious allergy, carry a written card in Korean as well.

FAQ

How do you call a waiter in Korea?

Say "jeogiyo" (excuse me) or press the call button on your table.

How do you ask for the bill in Korean?

Go to the counter and say "gyesan halgeyo". Most restaurants take payment at the counter rather than the table.

How do you say "not spicy" in Korean?

"An maepge juseyo" (안 맵게 주세요) means "not spicy, please".
