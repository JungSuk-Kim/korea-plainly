Naver Map vs Google Maps in Korea

Google Maps is familiar, but Naver Map can be extremely useful in Korea. Here is how to use both without making your trip complicated.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Why use Naver Map?

Naver Map has detailed local information and is especially useful for finding Korean businesses, entrances, subway exits and walking routes.

What about Google Maps?

Google Maps is still useful for saving places, checking familiar travel information and keeping your trip organized.

The simple approach

You do not have to choose only one. Use Google Maps for your saved travel plans and Naver Map when you need detailed local navigation.

Tip: Before leaving for Korea, install both and make sure you can search for your hotel and major destinations.
