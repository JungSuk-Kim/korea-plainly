Naver Map vs Google Maps in Korea

By Korea Plainly · Published 7 October 2026 · Updated 13 October 2026

Why Google Maps is limited in Korea and how to use Naver Map or KakaoMap instead: walking directions, subway exits, English settings and search tips.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

If you are used to Google Maps, Korea can be a surprise. For years Google Maps has been unable to give full walking and driving directions here, so most locals and many visitors use Naver Map or KakaoMap instead. This guide explains why, what each app is good for, and how to set them up before your trip.

Why Google Maps is limited in Korea

Korean law restricted taking detailed map data out of the country, so Google could not offer the same navigation it does elsewhere. In February 2026 the Korean government conditionally approved Google's request to export map data, which should eventually allow full walking and driving directions. Until those features are working on your phone, it is safest to travel with a Korean map app as well.

Today Google Maps is still useful for:

  • public transport directions by subway and bus,
  • finding and saving places, and reading reviews in English,
  • keeping the trip plan you made at home in one place.

What Naver Map does better

  • Walking directions that follow real paths, crossings and underpasses.
  • Subway exits: it tells you which numbered exit to use, which saves a lot of wandering at big stations.
  • Live bus arrival times and accurate transit routes, including the best carriage for transfers in some stations.
  • Up-to-date local listings: opening hours, menus, break times and photos for restaurants and cafes, often more complete than Google's.
  • English interface: you can switch the app language to English in the settings, and search for major places in English.

KakaoMap is the main alternative, with similar transit and walking directions. Some people prefer its interface; it is worth having as a backup.

How to set up Naver Map

  1. Install Naver Map before you leave and switch the language to English in the settings.
  2. Search for your hotel and save it, so you can always get back.
  3. Save the places on your list. Searching in English works for well-known places; for smaller ones, paste the Korean name or address.
  4. Allow location access so it can show where you are and guide you on foot.

Tips for searching

  • Copy the Korean address from your hotel booking or the place's website. It is the most reliable way to search.
  • If an English search finds nothing, try the romanised Korean name, or paste the name in Korean.
  • Use the station exit number shown in the route: "Exit 3" is often the difference between a two-minute and a ten-minute walk.
  • Check the opening hours and break time in the listing before you go, as many small restaurants close in the afternoon.

The simple approach

You don't have to choose. Plan your trip and save places in Google Maps if you like, then use Naver Map or KakaoMap to get from A to B on the ground. Every Korea Plainly place guide includes a Naver Map link for this reason.

FAQ

Does Google Maps work in South Korea?

Partly. Place search and public transport directions work, but walking and driving directions have been limited. Korea approved Google's map data request with conditions in 2026, but check whether full directions are available before relying on them.

Is Naver Map available in English?

Yes. Change the language in the app settings, and search for major places in English.

Naver Map or KakaoMap?

Both work well. Naver Map is the most widely used by visitors; KakaoMap is a good backup.
