Planning a trip to Gangneung

Getting there

The KTX runs from Seoul Station and Cheongnyangni to Gangneung in about two hours. Express buses from Seoul take a little longer.

How long to stay

One day covers Gyeongpo Beach and lake, Anmok's coffee street and Ojukheon; stay overnight for a sunrise at Jeongdongjin or Gyeongpo.

Getting around

City buses and taxis connect the station, beaches and sights. Distances between the beaches are short by taxi.

When to go

Summer is beach season. Winter is cold but bright, and the east coast is the classic place to watch the first sunrise of the year on 1 January. Spring brings cherry blossoms around Gyeongpo Lake.

Where to stay

Stay near Gyeongpo Beach for the sea and the lake, or near the station for easy trains.
