Do You Need Cash in Korea? Cards, ATMs and Payments

Korea is highly card-friendly, but carrying some Korean won is still useful. Learn when to use cards, cash and ATMs.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Can you use cards?

Yes. Cards are widely accepted in Korea, especially in shops, restaurants and hotels. Contactless and chip payments are common, but the exact options can vary by business.

Why carry cash?

A small amount of Korean won is useful for situations where cards are inconvenient, small purchases or topping up transportation cards.

Using ATMs

International cards can work at some ATMs, but availability and fees vary. Check your bank's overseas withdrawal policy before your trip.

Good balance: Use your card for most purchases and keep a modest amount of Korean cash as backup.
