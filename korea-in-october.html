Korea in October: Seoul, Busan and Jeju Weather and What to Pack

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 16 October 2026 · Updated 25 October 2026

Seoul, Busan and Jeju weather in October, with average temperatures, what to pack, when the autumn leaves turn, festivals and busy holiday dates.

Photo: Tung Thanh Dang, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

October is often called the best month to visit Korea. The summer heat and the rainy season are long gone, skies are usually clear, the air is crisp and the autumn leaves begin to turn. The catch is that everyone knows it: hotels, palaces and mountain trails are busy, especially at weekends. This guide covers Seoul, Busan and Jeju weather in October, what to pack, and how to plan around the crowds.

What the weather is like

October is the month when Korea swaps summer for autumn. In the first week, afternoons can still feel like late summer; by the last week, mornings are cold enough for a proper jacket. It is one of the driest and sunniest months of the year, so a rained-off day is unusual, though a passing shower or a late typhoon on the south coast is not impossible early in the month.

Seoul in October

Seoul averages daytime highs of about 20°C and lows of about 10 to 11°C across the month, but the spread is wide: early October highs are often in the low to mid twenties, while late October can bring mornings of 5°C or below. Rainfall is low, about 50 mm in the whole month, on roughly five rainy days. The big gap between the warm afternoon and the cold evening catches many visitors out, so plan your clothes around the evening, not the midday sun.

Busan in October

Busan is a few degrees milder than Seoul, with average highs of around 22°C and lows of around 15°C. The sea is still relatively warm, so the coast feels pleasant for walks along Haeundae, Gwangalli and the Haeparang Trail, though it is too cool for most people to swim. Our things to do in Busan guide has ideas for a few days there.

Jeju in October

Jeju is similar to Busan, with highs of about 22°C and lows of about 16°C, but it is windier and the weather changes faster, especially on Hallasan, where the summit is far colder than the coast. October is a good month for Jeju's Olle walking trails and the silver eulalia grass on the oreum (small volcanic hills), which turns the slopes pale and feathery. See our Jeju itinerary.

These figures are long-term averages (1991–2020) from the Korea Meteorological Administration; real weather varies from year to year, so check the forecast in the week before you fly. The mountains are always colder than the cities: on high peaks such as Seoraksan, the first frost can arrive in mid October. For the rest of the year, see our seasons and weather guide.

What to pack

Pack for two seasons in one day. A typical October day in Seoul starts cool, warms up by lunchtime and turns chilly again after sunset, which comes at around six o'clock and earlier as the month goes on.

  • Early October: T-shirts or light shirts, a thin long-sleeved layer and a light jacket or cardigan for the evening.
  • Late October: a sweater or fleece and a warmer jacket. A light down jacket that packs small is useful if you plan early starts or evenings by the Han River.
  • Good walking shoes: palaces, markets and hiking trails all mean a lot of walking. Proper hiking shoes are worth it for mountain trails, which are steep, rocky and slippery with fallen leaves.
  • Sunglasses and sunscreen: clear autumn days are bright, especially on a hike.
  • A small umbrella: rain is rare, but convenience stores sell cheap ones if you are caught out.
  • Lip balm and hand cream: the air gets drier as autumn goes on.

Things to do in October

Catch the first autumn leaves

The colours start at the top of the high mountains, such as Seoraksan, around the end of September and the first half of October, then move south and downhill. Most city parks, palaces and temples are at their best from late October into early November. If you visit early in the month, head for the mountains; if you visit late, Seoul's palaces and Namsan are lovely. Our autumn leaves guide has the usual timing for each area.

Go hiking or take a day trip

Cool, dry weather makes October the most comfortable month for hiking. Bukhansan is reachable by subway and bus from central Seoul; Nami Island, Gyeongju and Jeonju make easy day or overnight trips. Start early: famous trails and the trains to them fill up at weekends. Our guide to Jeonju Hanok Village from Seoul covers one easy trip.

Festivals

October is festival season. The Busan International Film Festival usually runs in early to mid October around Centum City and Haeundae. The Seoul International Fireworks Festival lights up the Han River at Yeouido on a Saturday evening in early autumn, and people claim their spot on the riverbank by mid-afternoon. The Jinju Namgang lantern festival fills the river with lanterns, and many towns hold food and harvest festivals. Dates change every year, so check our Korea festival calendar for this year's dates.

Evenings outdoors

Pleasant evenings are made for walking along Cheonggyecheon, picnicking at Han River parks (order fried chicken to the riverbank like the locals) or watching the city lights. Our Seoul night views guide lists the best spots.

Holidays and busy dates

  • 3 October, National Foundation Day: a public holiday. Shops and most sights stay open, but expect crowds.
  • 9 October, Hangul Day: a public holiday celebrating the Korean alphabet. Try our learn Hangul page before you go.
  • Chuseok: the harvest holiday follows the lunar calendar and falls in September or early October (in 2026 it was in late September). In a year when it lands in October, trains and roads are packed for several days, so book transport early. Big sights and shopping areas in Seoul mostly stay open, but some family-run restaurants and market stalls close.
  • When a holiday falls next to a weekend, Koreans travel too, and KTX seats, Jeju flights and hotels sell out. Check the calendar before you book.
  • Late October weekends: peak leaf-viewing season. Visit famous spots on weekdays, early in the morning, if you can.
  • Halloween weekend: busy nightlife areas such as Hongdae and Itaewon get crowded, and police now manage crowds closely. Avoid the narrowest alleys at peak hours.

FAQ

What is the weather like in Seoul in October?

Mild and dry. Average highs are about 20°C and lows about 10°C, with warmer afternoons early in the month and cold mornings by the end. Rain is light, around 50 mm for the whole month.

Is October a good time to visit Korea?

Yes, it is one of the best months: clear, dry and comfortable, with autumn leaves and many festivals. Book hotels and trains early, because it is also one of the busiest months.

What should I wear in Korea in October?

Layers. Early in the month a T-shirt with a light jacket is enough; by late October you will want a sweater and a warmer jacket, especially in the evening or in the mountains.
