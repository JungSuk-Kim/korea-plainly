Day Trips from Seoul: 8 Easy Trips by Train, With Autumn and Winter Picks

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 13 October 2026 · Updated 19 November 2026

Eight day trips from Seoul with the train or bus to take, the time each way and what to eat, plus the best picks for autumn leaves and winter lights.

Photo: Lance Vanlewen, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Seoul is big enough to fill a week, but a few of the trips locals actually take at the weekend are only one or two hours away: a fortress wall you can walk round in an afternoon, a port with the original jajangmyeon, lakes and river islands in Gapyeong, and the coffee coast at Gangneung. Here are eight that work for first-time visitors, with the train to take, what to eat, and the best picks for autumn and winter.

Suwon: Hwaseong Fortress

Getting there: Line 1 to Suwon Station, about an hour from Seoul Station; then a 10-minute bus or taxi to Paldalmun Gate. No booking needed, and your T-money card covers the subway.

Hwaseong is a late 18th-century wall, about 5.7 km round, that still circles the old centre of Suwon. The full loop takes two to three hours. A good half-loop starts at Paldalmun, climbs Paldalsan hill to the Seojangdae command post for the view, then drops down past Hwaseomun Gate to the Banghwasuryujeong pavilion above a lotus pond. Finish at Hwaseong Haenggung, the king's temporary palace, in the middle of town.

  • Eat: Suwon is known for galbi (marinated beef ribs) and for Tongdak Geori, the fried chicken alley near Paldalmun, where whole chickens are fried in big iron cauldrons.
  • Tip: the hill section is steep. Wear trainers, and in summer start early.

Incheon: Chinatown, Wolmido and Songdo

Getting there: Line 1 to Incheon Station (the end of the line), about 1 hour 10 minutes from Seoul Station. Chinatown's red gate is across the road.

Line 1 splits at Guro: one branch goes to Incheon, the other to Suwon and Cheonan, so check the destination shown on the front of the train. In Chinatown, the Jajangmyeon Museum tells how black bean noodles began here with Chinese port workers, and restaurants on the main slope serve the dish plus jjamppong (spicy seafood noodle soup). Walk up to Songwol-dong Fairy Tale Village, then take the small Wolmi Sea Train to Wolmido for a sea view. For a modern contrast, ride Incheon Line 1 to Central Park Station in Songdo.

Gapyeong: Nami Island and Chuncheon

Getting there: ITX-Cheongchun from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni to Gapyeong, roughly an hour; the slower Gyeongchun Line subway also goes there. A five-minute taxi reaches the Nami ferry wharf.

Nami Island is a small river island of tree-lined avenues, at its best in late October and early November when the ginkgo and metasequoia trees turn. The Gapyeong City Tour Bus links it with the Garden of Morning Calm (Achim Goyo Sumogwon) and Petite France. If you have energy left, stay on the ITX to the end of the line at Chuncheon for dakgalbi, spicy chicken stir-fried on a big iron pan at your table, and cold buckwheat makguksu. Our Nami Island and Gapyeong guide has the details.

Jeonju: hanok village and food

Getting there: KTX from Yongsan, about 1 hour 40 minutes; express buses from Central City Terminal take nearer three hours. Jeonju Station is a 15-20 minute taxi from the village.

Jeonju has more than 700 traditional houses in one neighbourhood. Walk to Gyeonggijeon Shrine, which holds the portrait of the founder of the Joseon dynasty, Jeondong Cathedral just opposite, and the Omokdae viewpoint above the rooftops. Eat Jeonju bibimbap for lunch and kongnamul gukbap (bean sprout soup) if you arrive early. It is the longest trip here; many people end up staying the night. See Jeonju Hanok Village from Seoul for trains, buses and a walking order.

Gangneung: coffee and the east coast

Getting there: KTX-Eum from Seoul Station or Cheongnyangni, about 1 hour 50 minutes.

Gangneung is where Seoulites go to see the sea without flying. Anmok Beach has a strip of cafes facing the water, Gyeongpo Lake is flat enough for a rental bike, and Ojukheon is the family home of the scholar Yi I and his mother Shin Saimdang, the two faces on the 5,000 and 50,000 won notes. For lunch, Chodang village serves sundubu, soft tofu traditionally set with seawater. The east coast is often clear and bright in winter, when Seoul is grey.

The DMZ

Getting there: on an organised tour from Seoul, or on Paju's own DMZ shuttle from Imjingak; about an hour each way.

A typical tour visits Imjingak park, the Third Infiltration Tunnel and Dora Observatory, which looks across to North Korea. Bring your passport: it is checked at the military checkpoint. The Joint Security Area at Panmunjom has been closed to ordinary tourists, so be wary of anything sold as a JSA tour. Our DMZ tour guide compares the options and the closing days.

Yongin: Everland

Korea's biggest theme park is about an hour from Gangnam by express bus, with the wooden roller coaster T Express, a safari and the giant pandas. Our Lotte World or Everland guide helps you choose.

Best day trips in autumn and winter

  • Autumn (mid-October to mid-November): Nami Island and the Garden of Morning Calm for yellow ginkgo; the Hwaseong wall walk in cool, dry weather; and Seoraksan near Sokcho, about 2.5 hours by express bus, with peak colour around mid-October. Our autumn foliage guide has the timing by region.
  • Winter (December to February): the Garden of Morning Calm's lighting festival, which usually runs from early December into March; Gangneung for sunshine and sea; and ski resorts such as Vivaldi Park, reached by shuttle bus from Seoul.

Tips for day trips

  • Book KTX and ITX seats ahead on the Korail Talk app or letskorail.com for weekends, Chuseok and Seollal; Friday evening trains out of Seoul fill up first. Our KTX guide explains how.
  • Use your T-money card for Suwon, Incheon and the Gyeongchun Line: they are on the Seoul subway network, so the fare is just distance-based. KTX and ITX need a ticket.
  • Check the last train back before you set out. Times and fares change, so confirm the day's schedule on Korail Talk and local routes on Naver Map or KakaoMap.

FAQ

What is the easiest day trip from Seoul?

Suwon and Incheon. Both are about an hour away on subway Line 1, you pay with your T-money card, and nothing needs booking.

What is the best day trip from Seoul in autumn?

Nami Island with the Garden of Morning Calm, from late October to early November, for the ginkgo avenues. Go on a weekday morning.

Can I visit Busan as a day trip from Seoul?

It is possible by KTX (about 2.5 hours each way), but it makes for a long day. Staying at least one night is much better; see our one day in Busan plan.
