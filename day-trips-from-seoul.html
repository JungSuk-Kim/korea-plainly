TRAVEL BASICS · DAY TRIPS

Easy Day Trips from Seoul for First-Time Visitors

Published 13 October 2026

The best day trips from Seoul and how to get there: Suwon's Hwaseong Fortress, the DMZ, Nami Island, Incheon, Jeonju and Gangneung.

Photo: Lance Vanlewen, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Seoul has plenty to fill a week, but some of Korea's best experiences are an hour or two outside the city: a UNESCO fortress, the border with North Korea, river islands, a port with a Chinatown, and Korea's biggest hanok village. Fast trains and good buses make all of these easy as day trips. Here are the best ones for first-time visitors, with how to get there.

Suwon: Hwaseong Fortress

Time from Seoul: about 1 hour by subway or train.

Hwaseong is a late 18th-century fortress wall that loops around the old centre of Suwon, and it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk part of the wall for views over the city, visit the rebuilt Hwaseong Haenggung palace, and finish at Suwon's famous fried chicken street near the market. Wear comfortable shoes: the full wall walk is several kilometres with slopes.

The DMZ

Time from Seoul: about 1 to 1.5 hours, by organised tour.

The Demilitarized Zone between North and South Korea is one of the most unusual places you can visit. Most sites, such as observatories and infiltration tunnels, can only be reached on a guided tour, and the Joint Security Area at Panmunjom has its own strict rules and sometimes closes. Book with a licensed tour company, bring your passport, and check the dress code and cancellation policy. Tours can be cancelled at short notice for security reasons.

Nami Island and Gapyeong

Time from Seoul: about 1.5 hours by train and bus, or by shuttle bus.

Nami Island is a small river island with tree-lined avenues, walking paths and riverside cafes, and it is at its best in autumn, when the ginkgo and metasequoia trees turn. Reach it by a short ferry or zipline from the mainland. Combine it with the Garden of Morning Calm, a large landscaped garden that is lit up in winter, or with nearby Chuncheon for dakgalbi, spicy stir-fried chicken. See our autumn foliage guide for the best timing.

Incheon: Chinatown and Songdo

Time from Seoul: about 1 hour by subway.

Incheon, Seoul's port city, makes an easy half or full day. Explore Incheon Chinatown, the birthplace of jajangmyeon (black bean noodles), and the colourful Fairy Tale Village next door. Then head to the modern district of Songdo for Songdo Central Park and its waterside skyline. If you are flying out, Incheon is also a good last stop before the airport.

Jeonju: hanok village and food

Time from Seoul: about 1.5 to 2 hours by KTX.

Jeonju is a long day trip but a rewarding one. Its hanok village has hundreds of traditional houses, and the city is famous across Korea as the home of bibimbap. Walk the village lanes, visit Gyeonggijeon Shrine, and eat bibimbap before the train back. Many visitors end up wishing they had stayed the night.

Gangneung: coffee and the east coast

Time from Seoul: about 2 hours by KTX.

Gangneung, on the east coast, combines beaches, history and a famous coffee culture. Visit Ojukheon, the birthplace of two of Korea's most famous historical figures, walk along Gyeongpo Beach and Lake, and drink coffee at a roastery like Terarosa or along Anmok Beach's cafe street.

Tips for day trips

  • Book trains ahead for weekends and holidays; KTX seats sell out. You can book on the Korail website or app.
  • Start early and check the time of the last train or bus back.
  • Use your transit card on subways and buses outside Seoul too. See our transit card guide.
  • Use Naver Map or KakaoMap for routes and bus times; they cover intercity trains and local buses.
  • Leave luggage at your hotel, or use a station locker. Long walks are much easier without bags.

FAQ

What is the easiest day trip from Seoul?

Suwon's Hwaseong Fortress and Incheon are both about an hour away by subway, with no booking needed.

Can I visit the DMZ on my own?

Most DMZ sites can only be visited on a guided tour. Book with a licensed operator and bring your passport.

Can I visit Busan as a day trip from Seoul?

It is possible by KTX (about 2.5 hours each way), but it makes for a long day. Staying at least one night is much better; see our one day in Busan plan.
