What it is

Haejigae Cafe sits in Aewol, on Jeju’s western coast, where the sea is a central part of the visit. It is a good choice when you want to slow down between sightseeing stops, enjoy a drink and something baked, and take in the coastal scenery without turning the outing into a full meal.

Aewol is known for its shoreline cafes and ocean-facing views. The area has a more leisurely feel than Jeju City’s busy centre, and many visitors come here to follow the coast, pause for coffee, and watch the light change over the water. Haejigae fits naturally into that kind of day. Its Google rating is 4.8 from 2,122 reviews, reflecting its popularity with visitors.

>

The name Haejigae evokes the setting: this is a place to look out toward the sea, especially as the day begins to soften. The appeal is less about rushing through a checklist and more about making time for the view. If you are exploring Jeju by car, it can make a pleasant rest stop along a coastal route; if you are staying nearby, it works as a simple destination in its own right.

What to see, do or order

Make the coastline your first focus. Choose a seat with a sea view if one is available, then give yourself time to look beyond the table: the water, changing clouds, and shifting coastal light are part of the experience. On a clear day, the western horizon is especially rewarding later in the afternoon, when the sun draws closer to the sea. Sunset conditions vary with weather and season, so treat the view as a bonus rather than a guarantee.

Haejigae is also described as a bakery cafe, making a drink and a baked treat a natural pairing. Check the counter for what is available on the day rather than planning around a particular item. Korean cafes often offer a broad choice of drinks alongside pastries or other desserts, but the selection can change. If you are visiting with someone who prefers a lighter stop, sharing a treat is an easy way to sample something without making the cafe visit feel like a full meal.

Take a few photos, but leave room to enjoy the place without viewing it only through a screen. The coastline gives you plenty to frame, and the atmosphere is best appreciated at an unhurried pace. If the cafe is busy, be considerate about holding a popular viewpoint or table for a long time after finishing. As in other Korean cafes, returning trays or sorting waste where the signs indicate is a helpful courtesy.

How to plan your visit

Allow around an hour for a drink, a bakery stop, and time to enjoy the view. Stay longer if you are building the cafe into a relaxed afternoon, but keep your plans flexible if you are touring several parts of the island. The address is 52 Aewolbukseo-gil, Aewol-eup, Jeju-si, Jeju-do. Having the Korean place name and address ready in a map app can help when navigating around Jeju.

Late afternoon is a particularly appealing time to visit if you want to see the coast in changing light. Midday can also suit a break from sightseeing, especially when the weather is clear. Jeju’s wind and coastal weather can shift, so the view may feel different from one visit to the next. On a rainy or hazy day, consider the cafe a comfortable pause rather than expecting a dramatic sunset.

Pair the stop with a walk along the Aewol shoreline or the well-known Handam Coastal Walk, then continue exploring the coast at an easy pace. You can also make it part of a wider west-coast drive, stopping at other viewpoints as the route allows. If you are travelling without a car, plan your transport before setting out; Jeju’s attractions are spread across the island, and a cafe stop is easiest when it fits your route rather than requiring a rushed detour.

Good to know

Haejigae Cafe is in Aewol, not in central Jeju City, so allow for the distance when arranging your day. The address is useful to save before you leave, especially if you are moving between coastal stops. Weather affects both the view and the comfort of spending time by the water, so bring a light layer if you plan to walk before or after your visit. Check current hours or prices on Naver Map before you go.

FAQ

Is Haejigae Cafe worth visiting for the view?

Yes. Its Aewol coastal setting makes the sea view a central reason to stop, particularly if you enjoy a slow cafe break by the water.

When is the best time to visit?

Late afternoon is a good choice for changing coastal light and the possibility of a sunset view. Clear weather gives you the best chance of seeing the horizon.

What can I pair with a visit?

Combine the cafe with a walk along the Aewol coast or the Handam Coastal Walk, or include it as a relaxed stop on a drive around Jeju’s west coast.
