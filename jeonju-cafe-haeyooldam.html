Haeyooldam in Jeonju: A Café-Looking Stop That May Actually Be a Barbecue Restaurant

Published 11 October 2026

In brief: Haeyooldam (해율담, also styled haeyooldam) is listed here under the café category, but available search results describe it as a charcoal-grill barbecue restaurant. If you are looking for coffee and cake, check the current listing before making a special trip. Google’s listing shows a 4.9 rating from 1,107 reviews.

Address: 16 Hyeonmu 1-gil, Wansan-gu, Jeonju, Jeonbuk State, South Korea.

What kind of place is Haeyooldam?

Haeyooldam is an easy place to misread. Search results describe its entrance and interior as stylish enough to suggest a café or wine bar, while other recent results identify it as a specialist in charcoal-grilled barbecue. That difference matters when you are choosing where to take a break: this may not be a conventional coffee shop, and you should not assume that it serves café-style drinks or desserts.

The information available points more strongly to a dinner destination than to a daytime café stop. If you want a charcoal-grill meal, Haeyooldam may be worth investigating. If you specifically want espresso, tea, pastries, or a quiet place to work, confirm those details directly through the current map listing before setting out.
