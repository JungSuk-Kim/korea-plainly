Kakao T: A Simple Taxi Guide for Visitors

How visitors can use Kakao T to find taxis, understand pickup points and make getting around Korea easier.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

What is Kakao T?

Kakao T is a popular Korean transportation app that can help you request taxis and see pickup information.

Why is pickup location important?

Large stations, shopping areas and busy streets can have several possible pickup points. Always check the map and driver information carefully.

Before you ride

  • Check the pickup point.
  • Confirm the destination.
  • Keep your phone available.
  • Follow the app's payment instructions.
