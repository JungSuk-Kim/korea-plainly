Korea in June: Weather, Rainy Season and What to Do

By Korea Plainly · Published 17 October 2026

Visiting Korea in June: early summer heat, when the rainy season starts, what to pack, beaches, hydrangeas on Jeju and indoor plans for wet days.

Photo: Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

June is the start of summer in Korea. The first half of the month is often warm and pleasant, and the second half brings humidity and the start of the rainy season. Prices are still reasonable before the big summer holidays. Here is what to expect.

  1. What the weather is like
  2. What to pack
  3. Things to do in June
  4. Holidays and dates

What the weather is like

Early June in Seoul is warm and usually sunny, with long days. As the month goes on it gets hotter and more humid. The rainy season, called jangma, usually reaches Jeju and the south in mid to late June and Seoul towards the end of the month. It does not rain all day every day, but there can be heavy downpours. See our seasons and weather guide.

What to pack

  • Light, breathable clothes and a thin layer for strong air conditioning.
  • A compact umbrella or light rain jacket. Convenience stores sell cheap umbrellas if you get caught out.
  • Sandals or quick-drying shoes for rainy days.
  • Sunscreen and insect repellent.

Things to do in June

Early summer by the sea

Busan's beaches begin opening for swimming in early summer, with lifeguards and full facilities by July. Evenings on the sand at Haeundae or Gwangalli are lovely in June. See our Busan guide.

Hydrangeas on Jeju

From mid June, hydrangeas bloom along Jeju's roads and temple gardens, a favourite photo spot before the summer crowds. See our Jeju itinerary.

Evenings outside

Long, warm evenings are perfect for the Han River parks, night markets and rooftop views. Our Seoul night views guide has ideas.

Plans for rainy days

Keep a few indoor options ready: museums, the big malls and underground shopping centres, cafés, a Korean bathhouse (see our jjimjilbang guide) or a Korean barbecue dinner (see our Korean BBQ guide).

Holidays and dates

  • 6 June, Memorial Day: a public holiday honouring those who died for the country. It is a day of remembrance, not celebration.
  • School summer holidays start in July, so June is quieter than July and August.

FAQ

When does the rainy season start in Korea?

Usually mid to late June in the south and on Jeju, and towards the end of June in Seoul. It normally lasts about a month.

Is June a good time to visit Korea?

Early June is a good time: warm, sunny and less crowded than midsummer. Late June brings humidity and rain.

Can you swim in Korea in June?

The sea is still cool in early June. Busan's beaches begin opening for swimming in early summer and are fully open with lifeguards by July.
