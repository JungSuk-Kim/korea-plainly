What to Know Before Visiting a Korean Bathhouse

A beginner-friendly explanation of what to expect at a Korean jjimjilbang or public bathhouse.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

What is a jjimjilbang?

A jjimjilbang is a Korean bathhouse and relaxation space. Some have separate bathing areas and shared lounge areas.

What should you expect?

Bathing areas generally require you to follow specific washing and clothing rules. Shared areas may have uniforms provided by the facility.

First-time advice

Follow posted instructions and watch what other guests do. Facilities vary, so check the rules when you arrive.
