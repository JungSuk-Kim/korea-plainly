Best Time to Visit Korea: Month-by-Month Guide and What Month Autumn Starts

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 17 October 2026 · Updated 2 November 2026

Best months to visit South Korea, what month autumn starts, blossom and leaf timing, holidays to plan around and quieter months, with a guide to every month.

Photo: GzarJH, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Korea has four distinct seasons, and the month you choose changes almost everything: what you wear, what you eat, how crowded the palaces are and even how much the hotel costs. Spring and autumn are the easy favourites, but every month has something going for it. Here is the short answer, what month autumn and spring actually arrive, and a quick look at all twelve months with links to their full guides.

The short answer

  • Best overall: mid April to May, and late September to early November. Mild days, low humidity and mostly clear skies.
  • Cherry blossoms: late March in the south (Busan, Jinhae, Jeju), early April in Seoul.
  • Autumn leaves: mid October in the high mountains, late October to mid November in Seoul and the southern cities.
  • Beaches and summer festivals: July and August, if you can handle heat and humidity.
  • Snow, skiing and ice festivals: late December to February.
  • Quieter and often cheaper: March, June, late November and January to early February, outside the big holidays.

What month is autumn in South Korea?

Officially, autumn in South Korea runs from September to November, but it does not feel like autumn on the first of September. Early September is often still warm and sticky, and typhoons can brush the south. The real change usually comes in the second half of September, when the humidity drops and mornings turn fresh.

October is the heart of the season: crisp, sunny and rarely rainy, which is why many Koreans call it the best month of the year. Leaves turn first on high peaks such as Seoraksan (usually mid October) and then work their way down and south. In Seoul, the ginkgo trees and palace maples are usually at their best from late October into the first week or two of November. By late November, the trees are mostly bare and Seoul's mornings can drop close to freezing.

If autumn colours are the reason for your trip, aim for the last week of October or the first week of November in Seoul, and check our autumn foliage guide for where to go. Timing can shift by a week or more from year to year.

When does spring start?

Spring runs roughly from March to May. March is still cool, especially in Seoul, where you will want a proper jacket. Blossoms move north through the country: forsythia and plum first, then cherry blossoms in late March in the south and around the first ten days of April in Seoul. May is warm, green and settled, though sunny days can reach the high 20s°C by the end of the month.

One thing locals check in spring is the fine dust (misemeonji) forecast. Some days in March and April are hazy, and many people wear a mask outdoors when the reading is poor. Weather apps and Naver show the daily level. See our cherry blossom guide for the best spots and expected timing.

Month by month

  • January: the coldest month. Dry, sunny and freezing in Seoul; ice fishing festivals and skiing.
  • February: still cold, with the Seollal holiday and the last of the ski season.
  • March: winter turns to spring; first blossoms in the south, chilly evenings in Seoul.
  • April: cherry blossom season and mild days; busy but beautiful.
  • May: warm, green and sunny, with lantern festivals for Buddha's Birthday.
  • June: pleasant at first, then humid as the rainy season (jangma) usually starts late in the month.
  • July: monsoon rain, then heat; beaches and summer festivals.
  • August: the hottest and busiest domestic holiday month; typhoons possible.
  • September: the heat fades; the Chuseok harvest holiday.
  • October: crisp, clear and colourful; the busiest festival month.
  • November: peak autumn leaves in the cities, then the first cold days.
  • December: Christmas lights, winter markets and the first ski days.

Holidays to plan around

The two big family holidays, Seollal (Lunar New Year) and Chuseok (the harvest festival), move with the lunar calendar. In 2027, the Seollal holiday runs from 6 to 9 February and Chuseok from 14 to 16 September. During these days:

  • KTX and express bus tickets between cities sell out weeks ahead, and motorways are jammed.
  • Many small family restaurants and traditional market stalls close for a day or more.
  • Palaces, big department stores, convenience stores and most chain cafés stay open, and central Seoul is pleasantly quiet.

Long weekends also matter. When a public holiday falls next to a weekend, Koreans travel, and places like Jeju, Gangneung and Gyeongju fill up. Early October and the start of May can also be busy because of holidays in China and Japan. Our Seollal travel tips explain what to expect, and the festival calendar shows what is on.

Weather at a glance

  • Seoul has the widest range: well below freezing on January mornings and over 30°C on August afternoons.
  • Busan is milder in winter and breezier in summer, and it rarely snows.
  • Jeju is the mildest in winter but windy and changeable; rain can come and go in an hour.
  • Gangwon's mountains (Pyeongchang, Seoraksan) are the coldest and snowiest, which is why the ski resorts are there.

Indoors is a different story: buildings and the subway are well heated in winter and strongly air-conditioned in summer, so layers work better than one heavy coat. For temperatures and what to wear, see our seasons and weather guide and the packing list.

Planning your trip

For a first visit, book flights and hotels early for April and October, as these are the most popular months with visitors from abroad as well as Koreans. If you are flexible, the weeks just before or after the peaks, such as late May or late November, give you much of the same weather with fewer crowds. FAQ

What month is autumn in South Korea?

Autumn is September to November. It feels most like autumn from late September, October is usually the clearest and most comfortable month, and Seoul's leaves tend to peak in late October to early November.

What is the best month to visit Korea?

April, May and October are the favourites for comfortable weather and blossoms or autumn colours. Late September and early November are great too, and often a little quieter.

When should I avoid visiting Korea?

No month is a bad choice, but plan around Seollal and Chuseok if you want to travel between cities, and expect heavy rain in July and strong heat in August.
