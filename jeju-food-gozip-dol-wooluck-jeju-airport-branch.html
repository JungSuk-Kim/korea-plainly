What it is

Gozip Dol Wooluck Jeju Airport Branch is a Korean restaurant at 30 Imhang-ro, on Jeju Island. Its name and local identity are closely associated with dol wooluck, or rockfish, and it is known as a seafood-focused place to eat. For a first-time visitor, it offers a chance to sit down for a substantial Korean meal rather than rely on a quick snack between sightseeing stops.

Jeju’s food culture is strongly connected to the surrounding sea. Seafood restaurants are a natural choice on the island, and a meal here can fit neatly into a day of exploring Jeju City or travelling to and from the airport. The branch’s airport-side location also makes it worth considering when you want a proper meal at the start or end of your island stay.

The restaurant has a Google rating of 4.7 from 5,873 reviews, a strong indication of its popularity with diners. Keep in mind that a busy, well-known restaurant may feel different from a quiet neighbourhood eatery, especially during popular meal periods.

What to see, do or order

The main reason to come is the food. Look for the seafood choices on the current menu, and ask which dishes feature rockfish or other local seafood if you want to try something associated with the restaurant. Recipes and available dishes can change, so it is better to choose from the menu on the day than to plan around a particular item in advance.

If you are unfamiliar with Korean restaurant menus, take a moment to check whether a dish is served as an individual portion or intended for sharing. You can point to a menu item when ordering, and simple questions such as “Is this for one person?” or “Can we share this?” help clarify portion sizes. If you have a seafood allergy or dietary restriction, tell the staff clearly before ordering; fish and seafood ingredients can appear in sauces and side dishes as well as in the main dish.

At a Korean meal, several small side dishes may accompany the main order. These are often shared at the table. Use your own chopsticks or spoon to serve yourself, and follow the pace of the meal rather than expecting every dish to arrive at once. If you are dining with a group, agree on the main order together and ask staff for help if the menu is difficult to navigate.

After eating, you can build the rest of your day around Jeju City. Yongduam Rock, the coastal rock formation known as Dragon Head Rock, is a familiar stop for visitors exploring the city’s shoreline. Dongmun Market is another well-known city destination for browsing local produce, snacks and everyday goods. Choose one according to your route and energy rather than trying to fit every stop into a short airport visit.

How to plan your visit

Plan for a sit-down meal, with extra flexibility if you are travelling with a group or visiting at a busy time. The time you need will depend on your order and how quickly you want to eat; avoid scheduling the meal right up against a flight, tour departure or other fixed appointment. If you are heading to the airport afterward, leave a comfortable buffer for getting there and completing your travel arrangements.

The restaurant is most practical when you are already exploring Jeju City, travelling near the airport, or arranging a meal around arrival or departure. For a sightseeing day, you might eat first and then continue to a city attraction, or visit a coastal stop before returning for a meal. Yongduam Rock and Dongmun Market are useful options to consider, but check your route and transport plans before combining them.

For the best fit, decide whether you want a full seafood meal or simply need a convenient place to eat. If you are visiting with children or a group, check the menu together and allow time to settle in and order. A little flexibility makes it easier to enjoy the meal without turning it into a rushed stop.

Good to know

The address is 30 Imhang-ro, Jeju-si, Jeju-do. Save the restaurant’s name in Korean or show the address to your driver if you need help navigating. Its official Instagram account is useful for checking recent updates. Check current hours or prices on Naver Map before setting out.

If you have a specific dietary need, communicate it before ordering rather than assuming a dish is suitable based on its name alone. For groups, confirm whether the portions are individual or shared so you can order an appropriate amount.
