WOWPASS Card in Korea: How the Prepaid Travel Card Works

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 19 October 2026

Learn how to get, load and use a WOWPASS card in Korea, including its separate payment and transit balances, and what to check before relying on it.

Photo: Christian Bolz (크리스티안 볼츠), CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

WOWPASS is a prepaid card designed for visitors to South Korea. You load Korean won onto it and use that balance to pay at many shops and other businesses that accept card payments. It can be a convenient way to manage travel spending, but it is not a bank account or a guarantee that every payment will work.

The card can also be used with T-money for public transport. The important detail is that WOWPASS payment funds and T-money funds are separate balances. Loading one does not automatically add money to the other.

How the card works

WOWPASS is a prepaid payment card. You add funds before spending, then present the card at a merchant's payment terminal, much as you would with another payment card. Purchases are deducted from the card's available payment balance.

Visitors can generally load the card with Korean won at a WOWPASS kiosk. Some kiosks also let you exchange supported foreign banknotes and add the converted amount to your card. Available services and accepted currencies can vary, so check the kiosk instructions or the WOWPASS app before making a special trip.

The WOWPASS app can help you check your card balance and transaction activity. App features, top-up methods and requirements may change. Review the current instructions in the app, particularly if you plan to add money without visiting a kiosk.

Getting and loading a card

  1. Find a current location. Use the WOWPASS app or official service information to check where cards can be issued or topped up. Do not assume that every station or airport has a working kiosk.
  2. Follow the kiosk instructions. Choose the relevant card or top-up option and read the displayed steps carefully. If exchanging cash, check the amount and exchange details shown before confirming.
  3. Check your balance. Confirm that the payment balance has updated before leaving the kiosk. Keep the card and any receipt until you are sure the load was successful.
  4. Set up the app if useful. The app can make it easier to review spending and manage the card. Check its current sign-up and verification requirements, and make sure you can access your account while travelling.

Carry some cash as a backup, especially when you first arrive or are travelling outside central areas. A kiosk may be unavailable, busy or temporarily out of service, and not every business accepts prepaid cards.

Using WOWPASS for purchases

Use the card at a merchant that accepts card payments, and make sure there is enough money in the WOWPASS payment balance. If a payment is declined, check the balance and try another payment method rather than assuming the shop does not accept cards generally.

Acceptance is not universal. Some businesses may not take cards, and some terminals or payment situations may not work with a particular prepaid card. Online purchases can also have different requirements from in-person payments. Keep another way to pay available for reservations, deposits or purchases where a failed transaction could cause a problem.

When paying, follow the terminal prompts and check the displayed amount. If you are offered a choice of currency, paying in Korean won is often the simpler option for avoiding a merchant's own conversion rate. Keep an eye on your balance in the app so you can top up before it runs low.

Using the card on public transport

The card's T-money function is separate from the WOWPASS payment balance. Before using buses or the subway, make sure the T-money balance has been loaded. A successful shop purchase or a high WOWPASS payment balance does not mean you have money available for transit.

Check the current instructions for adding T-money value. Transit top-ups may use different methods from WOWPASS card top-ups, and the available options can depend on where you are. If you are unsure, ask at a staffed transport information point or check the guidance at a top-up machine. Leave a little extra time to sort this out before travelling.

What to know before you rely on it

  • It is prepaid. You can spend only the funds available for the relevant balance. It does not work like a credit card with a line of credit.
  • There are two balances. Keep track of the WOWPASS payment balance and T-money balance separately.
  • Keep a backup payment method. Card acceptance, kiosk availability and top-up options can vary. A small amount of cash or another usable card can help if something goes wrong.
  • Protect the card and account. Treat the card like cash, keep it secure and use the app's current guidance if it is lost or stolen. Do not share account details or verification codes.
  • Check refund rules before loading a large amount. Procedures for unused funds, card closure or refunds may depend on current terms. Do not assume you can withdraw the remaining balance from any ATM.

Is WOWPASS useful for your trip?

WOWPASS can be helpful if you want a prepaid way to pay in Korean won and prefer to manage spending through an app. Its separate transit function can also be handy, provided you remember to load T-money independently. It may be less useful if you expect to rely on one card for every purchase, need a particular online payment to work, or want to avoid checking balances.

Before travelling, check current kiosk locations, loading options and card terms. During your trip, keep the two balances distinct, confirm each top-up and carry a backup payment method. Those simple habits make the card easier to use and help prevent small payment problems from disrupting your plans.

Bottom line: A little preparation can make your time in Korea much easier. Keep this guide handy and enjoy exploring at your own pace.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WOWPASS a credit card?

No. WOWPASS is a prepaid card, so you add money before using it rather than borrowing from a credit limit. Your spending is limited to the available balance. Check the app or a kiosk to see your balance and plan a top-up if needed.

How can I add money to my WOWPASS card?

Use a WOWPASS kiosk to check the available top-up options and follow the on-screen instructions. The methods available may depend on the kiosk or transaction, so confirm the amount and currency before completing it. You can also check your balance in the app.

Can I use WOWPASS to pay for buses and subways?

The card can include a T-money function for public transit. Transit funds are separate from the WOWPASS payment balance, so having money for shopping may not mean you have money for a ride. Check your transit balance and top it up separately before traveling.
