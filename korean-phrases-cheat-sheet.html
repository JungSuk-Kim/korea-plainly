Korean Phrases Cheat Sheet: A Printable Page for Your Trip

By Korea Plainly · Published 16 October 2026

A one-page printable Korean cheat sheet for travellers: greetings, restaurant and café phrases, menu words, getting around, shopping and help, in Hangul with easy pronunciation.

Photo: travelmate2021 東大策略顧問有限公司, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Print this page, fold it into your passport or save it as a PDF on your phone. It has the Korean you are most likely to need on a trip, in Hangul so you can point at it, with an easy pronunciation guide. For more explanation, see our useful Korean phrases guide.

Tips for using it

  • Point, don't worry. Showing the Hangul is perfectly normal; people are used to it and happy to help.
  • Add 요 (yo) to be polite. Most phrases here already end in -yo or -nida, the polite forms you should use with strangers.
  • Use a translation app for the rest. Papago and the camera translation in Naver or Google work well for menus and signs.
  • Learn to read Hangul. It takes an afternoon and makes menus and signs much easier. Try our learn Hangul page with its memory game.

FAQ

How do I print this Korean cheat sheet?

Press the Print button near the top of the page. In the print window you can also choose "Save as PDF" to keep it on your phone.

Do I need to speak Korean to travel in Korea?

No. Signs in cities are usually in English too, and translation apps cover most situations. A few polite phrases still make a big difference.

What is the most useful Korean phrase for travellers?

"이거 주세요" (i-geo ju-se-yo), "this one, please". Point at what you want and you can order food, buy tickets and shop.
