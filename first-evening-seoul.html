SEOUL · FIRST TRIP

How to Spend Your First Evening in Seoul

A relaxed first-evening plan for visitors who have just arrived in Seoul.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Keep the first evening simple

After a long flight, there is no need to cross the entire city.

Start with your neighborhood

Walk around your hotel area, find a convenience store and have an easy first meal.

Add one nearby highlight

Choose one evening destination such as a market, shopping street or riverside walk.

Ideal first night: Check in → eat nearby → take a short walk → recharge for tomorrow.
