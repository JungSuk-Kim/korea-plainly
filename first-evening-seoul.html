SEOUL · FIRST TRIP

How to Spend Your First Evening in Seoul

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 7 October 2026 · Updated 15 October 2026

Just landed in Seoul? What to sort out, where to eat and which easy evening walks to take on your first night, plus getting back after the subway stops.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Photo: Taken by Charles Lam, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Your first evening in Seoul is usually a tired one. You have landed, crossed the city and checked in, and the best plan is something easy, close to your hotel and memorable enough to make you glad you came. Here is how to spend it well without overdoing it.

First, sort out three small things

Ten minutes now saves hours over the rest of your trip:

  • Data: make sure your eSIM or SIM is working, or that your pocket Wi-Fi is charged. Maps and translation apps depend on it.
  • A transit card: buy a T-money or similar card at a convenience store or station if you didn't at the airport. See our transit card guide.
  • Maps: install Naver Map or KakaoMap and search for your hotel, so you can always find your way back. Our map app guide explains why.

Our arrival checklist covers these in more detail.

Eat something simple and good

The easiest first dinner is whatever looks busy near your hotel. Korean restaurants that are full of locals at dinner time are rarely a bad choice. Some ideas that suit a tired first night:

  • A soup or stew restaurant: one dish per person, served with rice and side dishes. Easy to order and comforting after a flight.
  • Korean barbecue: great fun, but many places ask for at least two portions of meat, so it suits two or more people.
  • Chicken and beer (chimaek): Korean fried chicken with a cold beer is a classic evening meal and easy to share.
  • A convenience store feast: if you arrive late, a convenience store meal is a real option. See our convenience store meals guide.

Not sure how ordering works? Our guide to ordering Korean food covers tablets, call buttons and paying at the counter.

Take a short evening walk

Pick one walk near where you are staying rather than crossing the city: If you want a view of the city lights, our Seoul night views guide has the best spots.

  • Cheonggyecheon (central Seoul): a stream walk below street level, lit at night, and free. See our Cheonggyecheon guide.
  • Myeongdong: bright shopping streets with street food stalls in the evening. See our Myeongdong guide.
  • Hongdae: a student area with buskers, bars and late-night food, lively until late.
  • A Han River park: locals picnic by the river on warm evenings, often with instant noodles made at the riverside convenience stores.
  • Gwanghwamun and the palace walls: the plaza and the walls of Gyeongbokgung are lit after dark and quiet compared with the shopping districts.

Getting back to your hotel

The subway runs until around midnight, but last trains vary by line and station, so check your map app before you go out. After that, take a taxi. A late-night surcharge applies in the evening and early morning hours. Taxi apps made for visitors, such as k.ride, let you book in English and pay with a foreign card; see our taxi app guide.

Keep it gentle

  • Don't plan a big sight for your first evening. Palaces and most museums close in the late afternoon or early evening anyway.
  • Seoul is generally safe at night, but take the usual care with your belongings in crowded areas.
  • Drink plenty of water, and go to bed at a sensible local time to get over jet lag faster.
  • Use the evening to plan tomorrow. Our 3-day Seoul itinerary is a good starting point.

FAQ

Are restaurants open late in Seoul?

Many are, especially in busy areas, and some places and convenience stores are open 24 hours. Smaller restaurants may close earlier, so check before you set out.

Is it safe to walk around Seoul at night?

Seoul is generally considered safe at night, and central areas stay busy late. Use normal precautions, as you would in any big city.

What if I arrive very late?

Get to your hotel, pick up food and water from a convenience store, and save exploring for the next day.
