Korea Travel Mistakes That Are Easy to Avoid

Small mistakes can make a trip harder than necessary. These are easy things to know before visiting Korea.

Korea Plainly · 5 min read

Trying to do too much

Seoul is large. Packing too many distant neighborhoods into one day can mean spending more time in transit than enjoying places.

Relying on only one map app

Having both Naver Map and Google Maps available can be useful.

Ignoring transit time

Check the route before committing to several stops.

Overplanning meals

Some of the best meals happen when you notice a busy local restaurant while exploring.
