Luggage Storage and Delivery in Korea: A Practical Guide

By Jungsuk Kim, Korea Plainly · Published 6 November 2026

Find out where to store bags at Korean subway stations, airports and hotels, and how to arrange luggage delivery between the airport and your accommodation.

Arriving before hotel check-in or leaving Seoul after checkout? You do not have to drag your suitcase through a market or onto a crowded subway. In Korea, you can use self-service lockers, staffed luggage counters or a delivery service. The best choice depends on your bag size, route and how long you need to be without it.

Choose the right option for your day

For a few hours near one subway station, a locker is usually the simplest choice. For an oversized suitcase or a day trip across town, look for a staffed storage counter. Delivery is useful when you want to travel from Incheon Airport to your hotel without your bags, or move luggage between accommodation. In Korean, luggage storage is often called 물품보관 (mul-pum bo-gwan), and delivery is 짐 배송 (jim bae-song).

Think about your final stop before storing anything. A locker at Myeongdong Station is convenient for shopping around Jung-gu, but less useful if you plan to spend the day in Seongsu or Gangnam. Check the station and exit before you arrive, since a large station can have several concourses.

Use subway-station lockers for short stops

Many busy subway stations have self-service lockers marked 물품보관함. In Seoul, you may see them at Seoul Station, Hongik Univ. Station and Myeongdong Station. They are handy for a few hours of sightseeing, such as leaving a shopping bag before visiting N Seoul Tower or exploring Hongdae. Locker availability, sizes and payment methods vary by location.

Look for the T-Locker app, also known by the Korean name 또타라커, to search for participating lockers and check service details. Do not assume every locker is listed or that a large compartment will be free when you arrive. If the kiosk offers several language options, follow the on-screen instructions and keep the collection code or receipt. Take a photo of the locker number as well.

Station lockers are less suitable for a very large suitcase, a long stay or a bag you may need to open. Some stations have separate locker areas on different levels, so leave extra time to find the right one. At Seoul Station, for example, allow time to walk between the subway, KTX and AREX areas.

Look for staffed luggage storage

A staffed counter can be easier than a locker when your bags are bulky or you need help. Search for T-Luggage or other luggage-storage counters near central visitor areas such as Myeongdong, Seoul Station and Hongik Univ. Station. Service locations and booking options can change, so check current details on Naver Map or the service’s official site before setting out.

Staffed storage may suit a day spent between hotel checkout and an evening train. For example, you could leave a case near Seoul Station, visit Namdaemun Market, then return before boarding a KTX train. Ask whether the counter accepts your bag size and what time you must collect it; do not rely on a late pickup unless the service confirms it.

At the airport, store bags or send them ahead

Incheon International Airport has luggage-storage services in its terminals. Check the airport’s current service information for the counter location in Terminal 1 or Terminal 2, especially if you are connecting between terminals or flying from a different one. Airport storage is practical if you want to explore near the airport, but it may not make sense if your hotel is in central Seoul.

For a hands-free journey into the city, look into luggage-delivery providers such as ZIM CARRY (짐캐리). Depending on the service and route available for your date, you may be able to arrange airport-to-hotel or hotel-to-airport delivery. Book with the exact terminal, flight details and accommodation name. Give the hotel address in Korean if possible, and confirm whether the property accepts delivered bags before check-in.

On the AREX train from Incheon Airport, keep valuables and anything you need during the ride with you. A delivery service can save you from hauling a suitcase, but it does not replace your hand luggage.

Plan delivery carefully

Delivery is most useful when your itinerary has a clear handoff: for example, landing at Incheon, taking the AREX to Hongik Univ. Station and heading straight to a hotel in Mapo-gu. It is less convenient if you are changing hotels at short notice or have not confirmed that the receiving desk will be open. Read the provider’s rules for pickup deadlines, restricted items, bag labels and delivery times before paying.

Keep your passport, medication, wallet, electronics and travel documents in your day bag. Photograph each suitcase and its label, and save the booking confirmation and support contact somewhere you can access without your phone’s data connection. If a hotel name has similar Korean and English spellings, send the provider the exact Korean address from the hotel’s official information.

Useful Korean phrases

These short phrases can help at a counter or hotel desk:

  • 짐을 맡길 수 있나요? (jim-eul mat-gil su innayo?) — Can I leave my luggage here?
  • 몇 시까지 찾으면 되나요? (myeot si-kkaji chajeumyeon doenayo?) — What time do I need to collect it by?
  • 호텔로 짐을 보내고 싶어요. (hotel-lo jim-eul bonaego sipeoyo.) — I’d like to send my luggage to the hotel.

In Busan, the same options are useful around Busan Station, Seomyeon and Haeundae. If you are taking Line 1 from Busan Station or changing to Line 2 at Seomyeon, check where the locker sits in relation to your exit. A little planning means you can enjoy a bowl of dwaeji-gukbap or a walk along Haeundae Beach without wheeling your suitcase behind you.

Bottom line: A little preparation can make your time in Korea much easier. Keep this guide handy and enjoy exploring at your own pace.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose between luggage storage and luggage delivery?

Choose storage if you plan to return to the same place to collect your bags, such as a station or hotel. Delivery may be more convenient when you want your luggage sent to another location. Check the service’s coverage, collection process, and timing before deciding.

Should I reserve luggage storage or delivery in advance?

It depends on the provider and location. If you are traveling during a busy period or need a specific pickup or delivery time, check availability and booking requirements ahead of time. Confirm the drop-off and collection details, and keep any booking information accessible.

What should I keep with me instead of putting in stored luggage?

Keep your passport, wallet, phone, medication, valuables, and anything you will need before collecting your bag with you. Before handing luggage over, check the provider’s rules about restricted or fragile items. Make sure you know how to identify and retrieve your belongings.
